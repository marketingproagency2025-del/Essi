Meta and Google Ads Management With Every Lead Qualified

MarketingPro is a digital marketing agency running Meta and Google Ads management for businesses across Italy, Europe, the US and Albania. We plan the campaigns, test the creative, and then do the part almost nobody does: we call every lead ourselves and qualify it before it reaches your team.

An ad budget only counts when it comes back as signed work. Most of the waste in paid advertising hides between the click and the follow-up call, so we manage that stretch too. You are not buying traffic from us. You are buying conversations worth having.

What the service covers

  • Campaign strategy that starts from your commercial goal, not from a platform. The platform is a consequence of the goal, not the other way round.
  • Meta and Google, chosen per client. They do two different jobs: one creates demand, the other captures it.
  • Creative and audience testing, so decisions come from results rather than opinions.
  • Retargeting the people who already showed interest, who are the warmest audience you will ever have and the cheapest to convert.
  • The follow-up. Every lead is contacted and filtered by us before it reaches you.

Should you run Meta ads or Google Ads?

They do different jobs. Meta ads, across Facebook and Instagram, put your offer in front of people who were not looking for it yet, so they create demand. Google Ads catch people already searching for what you sell, so they capture it.

Most businesses need one more than the other, and the numbers show the split fast. We choose per client, based on your goal and your market, and we will say plainly if one platform is not worth your budget.

Meta ads results from seven real campaigns

These numbers are campaign exports, not projections. Across seven Meta campaigns we generated 6,150 leads from 1,084,529 people reached, at a blended cost of 1.87 euro per lead, with individual campaigns landing between 1.47 and 2.30 euro. Total ad spend was 11,480.98 euro, and every one of those leads was called and qualified before handover.

How we work

Start with the goal. Booked calls, quote requests, or sales. Everything downstream is chosen to serve that number.

Pick the platform to match. Meta finds people who were not looking yet. Google catches people already searching. Most businesses need one more than the other.

Test rather than guess. The same budget against the same audience can perform very differently depending on the creative. We find out which, rather than arguing about it.

Retarget the warm audience. People who already engaged are the cheapest conversions available, and the easiest to leave on the table.

Call and qualify every lead. This is the part that changes the economics. Your team stops spending its day on people who were never going to buy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms do you advertise on?

Most of our paid campaigns run on Meta, meaning Facebook and Instagram, and on Google. Which one leads depends on whether your customers are already searching for what you sell.

Do you really call every lead?

Yes. We generate the lead, then call and qualify it ourselves, so your team only speaks to people who are ready to buy or to ask for a quote. It is the reason our leads and other agencies leads are not the same thing.

How quickly will I see results?

The first two weeks rarely reflect what the third month looks like. Early spend buys information as much as leads, and campaigns get cheaper per result as the testing narrows.

How fast can you launch our ads?

Campaigns are typically live within days of the plan being agreed, and the first results report lands at 30 days.

How much does ads management cost?

We quote per project rather than publishing a rate card, because the right setup depends on your goal, your market and your budget. Tell us what you need and we will come back with a plan and a price, usually within one business day.
