# Italian (it) guide — MarketingPro

The Italian tree is the original native tree: the shipped it/ pages ARE the register
standard (informal `tu`, `Raccontaci il tuo obiettivo`, first person plural for what we
do). This file exists to pin what the pages alone cannot: target queries and terms.

## Fixed terms
- qualify family: `qualificare` (`chiamiamo e qualifichiamo noi`), never `filtrare`
  outside the homepage hero
- `funnel di vendita` (singular, ruled 49-3 in the corpus)
- `sponsorizzate` is the colloquial surface for paid social; keep it alongside
  `campagne pubblicitarie`
- Durrës in prose is **Durazzo** (the Italian exonym); schema keeps `Durrës`
- No em dashes anywhere (gate-enforced)
