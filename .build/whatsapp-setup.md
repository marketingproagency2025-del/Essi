# WhatsApp Business: stop the job-seeker inbound

The site changes set expectations and give you something to point at. **This is the
part that actually filters**, because the greeting message auto-sends to every new
contact before you read anything.

All of it is free, in the WhatsApp Business app. Nothing here needs a developer.

    Settings -> Business tools -> Greeting message / Away message / Quick replies

---

## 1. Greeting message (the important one)

Auto-sends to anyone who writes for the first time, and again after 14 days of
silence. Set **Recipients: Everyone**. Warm to a client, closed to a job seeker,
in one message.

**English**
> Thanks for contacting MarketingPro. Tell us about your business and what you need,
> and we will reply within one business day.
>
> This line is for client enquiries. We are not hiring and we do not accept job
> applications, freelance offers or outsourcing proposals. Those messages are not
> answered.

**Italiano**
> Grazie per aver contattato MarketingPro. Raccontaci la tua azienda e cosa ti serve:
> ti rispondiamo entro un giorno lavorativo.
>
> Questo numero e riservato alle richieste dei clienti. Non stiamo assumendo e non
> accettiamo candidature, offerte freelance o proposte di outsourcing. A questi
> messaggi non rispondiamo.

**Espanol**
> Gracias por contactar con MarketingPro. Cuentanos sobre tu negocio y que necesitas,
> y te respondemos en un dia habil.
>
> Esta linea es para consultas de clientes. No estamos contratando y no aceptamos
> candidaturas, ofertas freelance ni propuestas de externalizacion. Esos mensajes no
> se responden.

**Shqip**
> Faleminderit qe kontaktuat MarketingPro. Na tregoni per biznesin tuaj dhe cfare ju
> nevojitet, dhe ju pergjigjemi brenda nje dite pune.
>
> Kjo linje eshte per kerkesat e klienteve. Nuk po punesojme dhe nuk pranojme aplikime
> per pune, oferta freelance apo propozime outsourcing. Ketyre mesazheve nuk u
> pergjigjemi.

**Which language?** WhatsApp allows only one greeting. Use English if you want one
message for everyone, or Italian if most inbound is Italian. A short bilingual version
works too: the first line of English plus the first line of Italian.

## 2. Quick reply (for the ones who write anyway)

Shortcut `/nohire`. One keystroke instead of composing a reply:

> Thanks for reaching out. We are not hiring and we do not take freelance or agency
> proposals, so we will not be following up. Best of luck.

Then **label** the chat and **block** if they persist. Labels make the pattern visible.

## 3. Away message

Outside 09:00-17:00, Monday to Friday, matching the hours the site publishes in its
schema. Same body as the greeting, plus "we will reply on the next business day".

---

## What this will not fix

Most of this outreach is scraped from your **Google Business Profile**, Instagram, or a
bought lead list, never from the website. Two things help there:

- **GBP -> Messages**: turn chat off entirely if the volume is not worth it, or keep it
  and mirror the greeting text above in the automated FAQ.
- **Instagram**: Settings -> Privacy -> Messages, set message requests from people you
  do not follow to go to a separate folder rather than your inbox.

Blocking is not rude and it is not lost business. Nobody who wants to hire you sends a CV.
