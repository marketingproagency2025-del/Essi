We Have Opened a Representative Office in Riccione

MarketingPro has opened a representative office in Italy, in Riccione. You will find it at Via Arti e Mestieri 3, 47838 Riccione (RN).

Our headquarters has not moved. It is in Albania, in the city centre of Durrës, and it is where our team works.

The Headquarters Stays in the City Centre of Durrës

The campaigns are run from Durrës, and it is from there that the team calls every person who writes to you, before the contact is passed on. The new office changes none of that.

Riccione is another office, a representative one. It adds a place to meet in Italy and does not replace the headquarters.

For Businesses in and Around Riccione

The office is for companies in Riccione and the surrounding area that would rather discuss their marketing face to face. At a meeting you can tell us what you sell, who you want to reach, and what has worked for you so far and what has not.

If you work with us from elsewhere in Italy or from abroad, nothing changes. We talk by phone, email or WhatsApp, and your campaigns are still run by the team in Durrës.

Arranging a Meeting

The office is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, and on Saturday from 9 AM to 12 PM. Send us a message or give us a call before you come, so we can agree on a time.

The address with a map link, the phone numbers and the email are on the Riccione office page. If you would like to tell us about your business first, use the contact page.
