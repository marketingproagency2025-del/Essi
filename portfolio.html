Client Work: Fly System, OFYR Italia and Rika

Three businesses in three markets, running the same model. We look after the channels, produce what goes on them, and call and qualify every lead before it reaches the people who sell.

Fly System

Windows, doors and outdoor systems · Italy

Fly System supplies doors, windows, floors and outdoor systems across Italy. We run the social channels and the paid campaigns, and we produce the photography and video that fills them, from the cutaway that explains a window profile to the finished pergola on a terrace.

Every lead is called and qualified by us before it reaches their sales team, which is the same model we run for every client on this page.

Their website is one of ours, and it is live now at flysystem.io.

  • Social media management
  • Meta ad campaigns
  • Photo and video production
  • Lead qualification and follow-up
  • Website design and build

See the feed on Instagram, @flysystem.it

OFYR Italia

Outdoor cooking · Milan, Italy

OFYR Italia sells outdoor cooking, and it is a product that only makes sense in use. So the content is built around chefs working over live fire, food coming off the plate, and the events where people can taste it rather than read about it.

We run the channels and the campaigns, produce the photography and video, and qualify every enquiry before handing it over.

  • Social media management
  • Meta ad campaigns
  • Photo and video production
  • Lead qualification and follow-up

See the feed on Instagram, @ofyr_italia

Rika

Wood and pellet stoves · Italy

Rika makes wood and pellet stoves. We produced the company film for its Premium Store: the drone approach over the building, then the showroom itself, the range along the floor, and the details that actually sell a stove in person, which are the fire behind the glass, the weight of the stone and the controls.

Alongside the film we run the same stack as the others, from the channels and the campaigns through to the follow-up.

  • Social media management
  • Meta ad campaigns
  • Photo and video production
  • Lead qualification and follow-up
