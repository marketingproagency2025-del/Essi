A Sales Funnel That Closes, Not Just Collects

MarketingPro is a digital marketing agency that designs and builds sales funnels for businesses across Italy, Europe, the United States and Albania. We map every step from a stranger seeing your ad to a customer signing, then close the gaps where people fall out.

Leads rarely die at the start or the end. They die in the unwatched middle, between the first click and the follow-up call, and that middle is exactly what we build. The aim is a route that converts every month, not one lucky one.

What the service covers

  • The top of the funnel, where attention is earned and strangers first hear of you.
  • The middle, where interest becomes trust and most funnels quietly fail.
  • The bottom, where the decision is made easy rather than left to the buyer to figure out.
  • Leak diagnosis. The money is usually hiding in the step nobody is watching.
  • Follow-up, which is unglamorous, and which is the single highest-return part of the whole thing.

What does a sales funnel agency actually build?

First, a map of what you already have, because every business has a funnel whether anyone designed it or not. Then the missing pieces: the middle steps that turn interest into trust, the answers to the objections that stall a decision, and the follow-up that reaches a lead while it is still warm.

The build ends with handover rules. Every lead that enters the funnel is called and qualified by us before your team sees it, so the pipeline carries buyers rather than browsers.

How we work

Map what exists now. Before adding anything, we trace the route a real enquiry takes today, including the parts nobody designed.

Find the leaks. A funnel rarely fails everywhere. It usually fails at one identifiable step, and fixing that beats rebuilding the whole thing.

Build the middle. The stage between interest and decision is where most funnels have nothing at all.

Make the decision easy. Remove the friction, the ambiguity and the unanswered objection at the point of commitment.

Follow up properly. A lead is often only interested for a short window. Replying in minutes rather than days changes the outcome more than any other single change.

Who we build sales funnels for

We run this service today for Fly System, OFYR Italia and Rika. One of them makes windows and outdoor products in Italy, one sells outdoor cooking gear, and one makes wood and pellet stoves for the Italian market.

The follow-up systems behind our campaigns have handled 6,150 leads across seven Meta campaigns, each one called and qualified before handover.

Who this suits

Businesses already generating enquiries that are not converting. If leads arrive and go nowhere, the problem is usually the funnel rather than the traffic, and buying more traffic will simply cost more to waste.

Timelines and pricing

Timelines and pricing depend on scope, so we quote per project rather than publishing a rate card. Tell us what you need and we will come back with a plan and a price. We usually reply within one business day, and often the same day.

For a sales funnel, the mapping and build typically take about a week.

Frequently Asked Questions

What actually is a sales funnel?

The route someone takes from first hearing about you to becoming a customer. Every business already has one, whether or not anyone designed it. The question is only whether it works.

Do I need one if my ads already work?

Especially then. Ads that produce leads a funnel cannot convert are the most expensive kind, because you are paying full price for a result you never collect.

How fast does follow-up need to be?

Fast. Interest decays quickly, and replying in minutes rather than days changes the outcome more than almost anything else you could adjust.

How much does a sales funnel cost?

We quote per project, because the price depends on what already exists and how much of the route needs building. Tell us what you have and we will come back with a plan and a price, usually within one business day.

How long does it take to build a sales funnel?

A funnel is typically mapped and built in about a week. The tuning continues after launch, because the first real leads always show you something the map did not.
